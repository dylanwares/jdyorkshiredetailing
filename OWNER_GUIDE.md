# Looking after your website

You can change your prices and your photos yourself. You don't need to log in to the website, and it's hard to break. If something goes wrong, the site keeps showing the last version that worked.

## Prices: the Google Sheet

Your price list is the **Prices** tab of your Google Sheet. Whatever is in that sheet appears on the Prices page.

| Column | What to put in it |
|---|---|
| **category** | The section heading, e.g. `Valets`. Rows with the same category are grouped together. |
| **service** | The name, e.g. `Mini Valet`. If this is blank, the row is ignored. |
| **description** | A short line about the service. You can leave it blank. |
| **price** | A number, e.g. `35` or `£35`. Leave it blank to show **"Contact for quote"**. Don't type words here (like "from £35"), or the row won't show. |
| **active** | `TRUE` shows the service. `FALSE` hides it. |
| **order** | `1`, `2`, `3`… sets the order within its category. |

- **To change a price:** type the new number.
- **To hide a service for now:** set `active` to `FALSE`. Set it back to `TRUE` to show it again.
- **To add a service:** add a new row and fill in every column.

**Please don't change** the top row of column names, or the tab name `Prices`. Either one stops the site reading your prices. If that happens, the site keeps showing the last prices it read successfully.

**Changes take 10 to 25 minutes to appear** on the site. You don't need to do anything else. If you still see the old price after that, try refreshing the page.

## Photos: the Google Drive folder

Every photo in your shared **gallery folder** in Google Drive appears on the Our Work page, newest first. The latest 6 also appear on the home page.

- **To add photos:** upload them to the folder. They appear within about 30 minutes.
- **To remove a photo:** delete it from the folder.
- **Use JPEG photos, not HEIC.** On an iPhone, go to *Settings → Camera → Formats* and choose **Most Compatible**. HEIC photos won't appear on the site.
- Videos and other files in the folder are ignored. So is the price sheet.
- **Don't change the folder's sharing setting.** It has to stay on "Anyone with the link can view".

## Before and after slider

The home page can show a **Before & after** slider: visitors drag a handle to compare two photos of the same car. It uses photos from a subfolder of your gallery folder.

1. In your gallery folder, create a subfolder called **Before and After**.
2. Upload each pair with **the same name**, ending in `-before` and `-after`. For example, `audi-a3-before.jpg` and `audi-a3-after.jpg`. The name in front of `-before` or `-after` is shown under the slider (`audi-a3` appears as "Audi A3").
3. Wait about 30 minutes for it to appear on the home page.

- **A photo without a partner isn't shown.** If you upload `audi-a3-before.jpg` but no `audi-a3-after.jpg`, neither appears until the second photo is added.
- The latest **4 pairs** are shown. To remove a pair, delete either photo.
- With no pairs in the folder, the section doesn't appear at all.
- For the best result, take both photos from the **same angle and distance**, in landscape (sideways), and use JPEG, like the gallery photos. Photos are shown in a 4:3 frame, so the edges of very tall photos get cropped.

## Enquiries

When someone fills in the form on the Contact page, the enquiry is **emailed to you** straight away. If the phone alert is set up, you'll get a **Telegram message** too. Reply to the email to reply to the customer, if they left an email address. Their phone number is always included.

**If enquiries seem to have stopped:**
1. Check your email's spam or junk folder.
2. Fill in the form yourself, as a test.
3. If your test doesn't arrive, contact Dylan (below).

If sending fails, customers see a message asking them to phone you, so you won't lose work without knowing.
